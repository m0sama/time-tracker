# Problem statement

I work on several personal projects and do not have a reliable record of how much time goes into each one. Without it I cannot tell which projects are getting attention, cannot estimate future work, and cannot reconstruct what I did last week.

Existing tools are built for teams and billing, with accounts, integrations, and dashboards. For one person logging their own time, that is too much to set up and maintain.

**Cost of the problem:** unclear priorities and vague memories of effort. Time spent on one project quietly crowds out others.
